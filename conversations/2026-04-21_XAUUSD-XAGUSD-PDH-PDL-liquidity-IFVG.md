## 1. XAUUSD vs XAGUSD — Inter-Market Divergence

**Setup observed:**
- XAUUSD swept PDH ✅
- XAGUSD failed to sweep PDH, but swept PDL instead

### Insight
This is a classic **bearish inter-market divergence**. When Gold and Silver fail to confirm each other, it signals weakness rather than a clean trend.

- XAUUSD's PDH sweep = **liquidity grab** — stops above PDH triggered, then reversal
- XAGUSD sweeping PDL while failing PDH = **bearish institutional delivery** in Silver
- Silver often leads Gold — the PDL sweep in Silver warned that the real move was **down**
- **Bias: Bearish** on both metals short-term

### IFVG Analysis
The aggressive bearish leg through PDL/ASL created **bearish FVGs** on the way down. After the reversal, those bearish FVGs flipped to **IFVGs (Inverse Fair Value Gaps)** — acting as bullish launch pads on any retracement.

The fadi SMT indicator's blue box marks the IFVG zone just above ASL.
